## Projects (nights & weekends)

- Walk the Store (https://jeremyperonto.com/walk-the-store/): a free Claude skill that audits your storefront by shopping it as a real, qualified customer. The digital version of one of my favorite practices at Amazon, seeing the site experience through a customer's perspective.
- Mariners Meter (https://jeremyperonto.com/MarinersMeter/): a visualization for what it feels like to be a Seattle baseball fan. Live game stats and the win-loss record, mapped back to the start of the franchise.
- Della's Doll Company (https://jeremyperonto.com/dellas-doll-company/): a simple interactive game I built for my daughter to teach her about what goes into making a toy. Design, manufacture, ship, and sell a doll.
- Derby Dash (https://jeremyperonto.com/derby/): a Pinewood Derby simulator I built for my son. Carve a racer, race it in 3D, learn the physics that make it fast, and print out a plan to make it in real life.
